[[Ecosystem]]s live within [[biomes]]. This is true for both [[land ecosystems]] and [[terrestrial biomes]] as well as [[aquatic ecosystem]]s and [[aquatic biomes]].

For example, a terrestrial biome would be a rainforest. Within that terrestrial biome, a land ecosystem would be the Amazon rainforest, and within the Amazon rainforest, a [[microecosystems|microecosystem]] would be a rotting log.
