In oceans, ocean currents move water around the Earth, regulating temperature and maintaining equilibrium.  However, in the process of ocean currents moving water, it can change [[abiotic factor]]s in different [[environment]]s, changing the environmental conditions of those environments and affecting the [[species richness]] of that environment.
