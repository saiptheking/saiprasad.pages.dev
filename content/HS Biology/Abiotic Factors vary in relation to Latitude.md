## Temperature decreases with [[latitude]]

- Places near the equator tend to be warmer, as sun's rays strike this place more directly
- Vice versa, places far from the equator are cooler

## Precipitation decreases with [[latitude]]

- Since temperature decreases, precipitation decreases, as temperature affects the amount of water vapor that air can hold
- Thus, equator regions hold more water, while polar regions hold less
